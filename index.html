01 — The Artist

More than
an image.

My work begins with moments that deserve to remain alive.

These artworks are rooted in drawing, imagination and personal memories. Each piece carries its own small world — a character, a story, a feeling or a moment that once mattered.

Turning these works into NFTs became a way of preserving those moments beyond their original time and place.

“Some moments are too meaningful to let them disappear.”
