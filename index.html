01 — Artist Story

More than
an image.

My work is rooted in memory, imagination, and the emotional moments that quietly become part of our lives.

Each artwork carries a story. These are not simply handmade images, but fragments of moments, feelings, and memories transformed into art.

Through NFTs, I chose to preserve these moments in a new form — so that the stories behind them can continue to exist and be discovered by collectors around the world.
